# Design

A shared length-preserving dash translation supplies typed date, resident identifier and month parsers; source words and string fields retain their original characters. Insurance issue selection is local to the known header table: recognize its label, bound the right cell by row and neighboring label, and accept a unique valid date there. Missing or ambiguous evidence remains absent through existing validation paths. NHIS header selection remains independent.

The diagnostic projection uses the same selection helper and serializes only a literal key allowlist with booleans, counts and predefined separator classes. File-mode dependency output and exceptions remain suppressed. Synthetic tests cover dash variants, misleading stamp dates, absent/corrupt cells and missing labels, merged/split OCR regions, evidence preservation, privacy, and compatibility. Replay the four deterministic Myeongjo cases with the original generator and seed.
